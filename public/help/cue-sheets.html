Tech cue sheets

A crew-ready sheet of Front of House and Lighting cues.

A tech cue sheet turns your setlist into a document your sound engineer and lighting designer can actually run the show from. Each song carries its Front of House and Lighting notes, plus transition cues between songs and notes for before and after the set.

The notes pull from each song's own FOH and Lighting fields, so you fill them in once and they show up every time. You can override any of them for a specific show without changing the song, because the room in Cleveland is not the room in Austin.

Each set starts on its own page when printed, and a single song never splits across a page break and leaves its cues stranded. Open one from a saved setlist or from the show page, tweak as needed, and print or export it for the crew.

The tech cue sheet is part of the paid version. Pro and Road Warrior open it, and so does the trial.
