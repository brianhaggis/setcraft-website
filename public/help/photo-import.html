Photo import

Turn a photo of a handwritten setlist into a real one.

You scrawled the set on the back of a flyer in the green room. Snap a photo of it with Photo Import, and Setcraft reads the handwriting, matches the titles to songs in your library, and builds the setlist for you. You review the matches before anything is saved, so a misread "Snowmelt" never becomes "Snowman" without your say-so.

A line that is not in your library yet does not get dropped. You can turn it into a new song on the spot, with the title and short name filled in, so scanning an old setlist doubles as a quick way to build out your library.

It needs an internet connection, because the reading happens on Setcraft's server rather than chewing your battery. Photo import runs on the free trial and on any paid tier: five scans a month during the trial, twenty a month once you buy. A scan only counts when it reads something; a blurry photo that comes back empty is free. Your handwriting, for the record, is not as bad as you think. Probably.
