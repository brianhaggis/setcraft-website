Themes

Nineteen looks, one per artist, your call.

Setcraft comes in nineteen themes, from sober Dark and Light and a high-contrast option built for readability, through to a parade of genre looks: Yacht Rock, Synthwave, Kickflip, The Shire, Woodstock, Wake Up, and more. Pick one in Backstage, or right in the setup wizard, and watch the whole app reskin.

Each artist profile remembers its own theme. Your folk project can be all warm paper while your synth side glows neon, and switching acts switches the look. None of it changes a single thing about your data. It is purely so the app feels like your band, not a spreadsheet.

Setcraft is a Mac app for working musicians: build setlists, print them big enough to read from the kit, and keep every song your band plays in one place. Download it free for 30 days.
