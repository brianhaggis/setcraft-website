Free, Pro, and Road Warrior

What the free version does, and what each paid tier unlocks.

New installs begin with a free 30-day trial of the full app. After that, Setcraft settles into the free version or a paid tier. Here is what each one does.

The free trial

For your first 30 days you have the full app at Pro level: one artist profile, your whole library, full-length setlists, photo import (five scans a month), tech cue sheets, PRO reports, Bandsintown sync, and audio attachments with transition previews. No card, no account. Printed and exported sheets carry a light trial mark, and exporting a portable backup stays held back, until you buy a license. A clean, gig-ready sheet is what a license unlocks. Build real sets, take them to a real gig, and decide once you know.

The free version

When the trial ends without a purchase, Setcraft becomes the free version: a real, working app, not a dead end. Everything you built stays, and stays printable.

  • You keep every song you already have, and you can add new ones until your library reaches 15.
  • A new setlist is capped at 15 songs.
  • Exports carry a Setcraft watermark overlay across each page.
  • Photo import, Bandsintown sync, tech cue sheets, PRO reports, audio attachments, transition previews, and portable backup export are paid features.
  • Your primary artist profile and the demo band stay available. Extra profiles need a paid tier.

Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase. It unlocks one artist profile, fully:

  • Your whole library, however many songs that is.
  • Full-length setlists.
  • Clean, unwatermarked printouts and PDFs.
  • Photo import and Bandsintown sync.
  • Audio attachments and transition previews.
  • Exporting a portable backup to carry your library to another Mac.

Road Warrior

Road Warrior is everything in Pro, with unlimited artist profiles instead of one. It is the tier for anyone running more than one act: a band, a side project, and a covers gig, each its own separate world.

Already on Pro and want more bands? Upgrade to Road Warrior from inside the app and pay only the difference. Your library carries straight over. See Activating your license.

The demo band ignores the free limits on purpose, so you can try the full experience before you spend a cent. Use it as your test bench, then come back and buy when you are convinced.
