The PRO report

Collect performance royalties for your own songs.

When you play your own songs live, you are owed performance royalties, and your performing rights organization (ASCAP, BMI, SOCAN, and the rest) pays them out if you tell them what you played. The PRO report builds that list for you from your shows and setlists.

Add each original song's registration number when you create it, or later in the song editor. The report gathers your originals, with their numbers and where you played them, into something you can submit. Covers are left off, because those royalties go to whoever wrote the thing.

It is the least glamorous feature in the app and quite possibly the one that pays for the app. Open it from a show with a setlist, or from the Shows list.

The PRO report is part of the paid version. Pro and Road Warrior open it, and so does the trial.
