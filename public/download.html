Which one do I have?

Open the Apple menu in the top left corner of your screen and choose About This Mac. Look for the line naming the chip or processor.

  • If it says Apple M1, M2, M3 or M4, take the Apple Silicon download.
  • If it says Intel, take the Intel download.

If you take the wrong one, nothing breaks. The app will simply refuse to open, and macOS may not explain why. Download the other one and it will work.

Installing

Open the disk image you downloaded and drag Setcraft onto the Applications folder. Drag it to Applications rather than running it from the disk image, so updates and backups have somewhere stable to live.

On first launch, if macOS blocks it, right-click Setcraft and choose Open once. It is signed and notarized, so that is a one-time step.

Moving to a new Mac

Download the version that matches the new Mac, then bring your library across with Backup and Restore. Your license moves too: if it says it is already in use, choose Deactivate all other computers on the Account & License tab, then activate on the new machine.

This comes up most when replacing an older Intel Mac. The new one takes the Apple Silicon download even though the old one took Intel. Same app, same license, same setlists.
